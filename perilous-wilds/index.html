Ask the Fates is an unofficial helper for The Perilous Wilds (Lampblack & Brimstone, 2015), written by Jason Lutes with Jeremy Strandberg, illustrated by Keny Widjaja. Its text is released under CC BY-SA 3.0; the tables reproduced here are used under that licence, and this page is offered under the same terms. Travel and camp procedures have been rewritten as system-neutral outcome prompts, so they work with whatever rules you're running. Buy the book — it's worth having at the table.
